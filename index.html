About

For 17+ years, I've helped tech and consumer companies navigate the space where product, policy, and public perception collide. I build narratives that mitigate risk and create meaningful connections between products and people.

My sweet spots are risk mitigation, product launch preparedness, data-driven storytelling, translating complex technical concepts into frameworks that resonate with everyday people, and developing high-performing comms teams.

After nearly two decades at the forefront of some of consumer tech's biggest moments, I took a career break to recharge, travel, and go deep on how generative AI is reshaping communications. I'm now exploring new opportunities.

Experience

2021 — 2025

Director, Head of Consumer, Product & Technology Communications @ Instacart

Built and led Instacart's Product & Technology Communications function, then expanded scope to oversee Consumer, Brand, and Shopper Communications — shaping the narrative that positioned Instacart as a technology company through its IPO and into its public company era.

Led comms strategy for Instacart's AI product portfolio, securing Tier 1 coverage in WSJ, Bloomberg, CNN, Fortune, and TechCrunch. Drove high-profile partnership announcements with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, TikTok, Condé Nast, and NYT. Served as primary comms partner to the COO, CPO, and Chief Architect.

AI Narrative Development Executive Comms Product Communications Data Storytelling IPO Communications
2014 — 2021

Product Communications @ Facebook (Meta)

Nearly seven years doing PR for consumer-facing products, with a focus on content distribution, algorithmic transparency, and trust initiatives during one of the most scrutinized periods in the company's history.

Led global comms strategy for Facebook's misinformation and disinformation mitigation efforts (2018–2021), coordinating with regional leads ahead of major elections in India, Indonesia, Brazil, and across the EU. Created Facebook's first cross-company "Integrity Day" press event. Managed publicists across News Feed, Groups, Search, and Trending.

Crisis Communications Algorithmic Transparency Issues Management Policy Comms Team Leadership
2007 — 2014

PR Manager, EA Mobile @ Electronic Arts (EA)

Cut my teeth launching 70+ video games at EA, navigating everything from product launches to crisis moments to longer-term thought leadership on the company's shift to mobile.
